# Support

## When can I reach technical support?
Technical support for business customers is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year for service outages and connectivity issues. Business Fiber 5 Gbps and 10 Gbps customers receive priority routing.

## When can I reach billing and account support?
Billing and account specialists are available Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern Time.

## How do I contact support?
Call the business support line printed on your welcome email and invoice, or reply to any Connectivity Max notification email. Business Fiber 10 Gbps customers can also contact their dedicated account manager directly.

## Do I get a dedicated account manager?
A dedicated account manager is included with Business Fiber 10 Gbps and assigned before installation begins. Other plans are supported by the business support team.

## How do I report an outage?
Report outages to 24/7 technical support. Outage time counts toward your SLA from the moment the ticket is opened.
