# Billing and payments

## Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover) and ACH bank transfers from checking or savings accounts.

## When is the first payment taken?
Payment is taken after your installation appointment is scheduled. That payment confirms the order.

## Can I pay in installments?
Yes. Payment plans with at least two installments are available on request, scheduled monthly, every two weeks or weekly, with the first installment due on or after the plan start date.

## Is my card information stored?
Card and bank details are tokenized; we never store or display full card numbers, only the last four digits.

## Do you run a credit check?
A business credit check may be run for new accounts. Depending on the result, an account is approved, approved with conditions such as a deposit, or referred for review.

## What happens if I don't complete payment?
Orders that are not paid within 48 hours of creation expire, and shopping carts expire after 24 hours of inactivity. You can start a new order at any time.
