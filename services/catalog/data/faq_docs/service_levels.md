# Service levels (SLA)

## What is your uptime guarantee?
Business Fiber 1 Gbps and Business Fiber 5 Gbps include a 99.9% uptime SLA. Business Fiber 10 Gbps includes a 99.95% uptime SLA, the strongest on our portfolio. SLA terms for other services are listed in each product's specifications.

## What happens if you miss the SLA?
If monthly availability falls below the guaranteed level, you can request a service credit on the affected service. Credits are applied to the next invoice after the outage is confirmed by support.

## How is uptime measured?
Uptime is measured per calendar month on the access circuit from our network to your demarcation point, excluding scheduled maintenance announced in advance.
