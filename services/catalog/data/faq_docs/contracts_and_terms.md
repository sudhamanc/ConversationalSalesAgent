# Contracts and terms

## What contract lengths do you offer?
Business services are sold on 12-month, 24-month or 36-month terms. A 12-month term is the standard term and has no term discount. A 24-month term earns a 5% term discount and a 36-month term earns a 10% term discount on the monthly price. We do not offer month-to-month business contracts.

## Is there a default term if I don't choose one?
Yes. Quotes default to the 12-month term unless you ask for 24 or 36 months.

## Do contracts auto-renew?
Contracts renew automatically for another term of the same length unless you give notice at least 30 days before the end date. Your renewal price is confirmed in writing before the renewal starts.

## Can I combine services on one contract?
Yes. Internet, Voice, SD-WAN and Mobile can be combined on one quote and one contract. Bundles earn a multi-product discount: Internet plus Voice 5%, Internet plus SD-WAN 7%, and Internet plus SD-WAN plus Voice 10%.

## How long is a quote valid?
Every quote is valid for 30 days from the date it is issued. After 30 days the quote expires and a new quote is generated at current pricing.
