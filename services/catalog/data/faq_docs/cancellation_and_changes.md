# Cancellation and changes

## What is your cancellation policy?
You can cancel an order at no charge any time before service is activated, as long as it has not been fulfilled. Once service is installed and activated, the order cannot be cancelled; the service is ended by terminating the contract instead.

## Is there an early termination fee?
Terminating an active contract before the end of its term incurs an early termination fee equal to 50% of the remaining monthly charges for the term.

## Can I change my order?
Orders can be modified before installation (for example upgrading from Business Fiber 1 Gbps to 5 Gbps). Changes after activation are handled as an upgrade on your account.

## Can I upgrade my speed later?
Yes. Upgrades are available at any time during the term; the new monthly price applies from the upgrade date and the term does not restart unless you choose a new term to earn a larger term discount.

## How do I cancel an installation appointment?
Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled any time before the technician is dispatched.
