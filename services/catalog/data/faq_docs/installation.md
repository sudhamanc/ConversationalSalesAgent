# Installation

## How long does installation take?
Most installations are completed in a single visit. Coax internet installs where cable is already in the building typically take 1 to 3 hours. Fiber installs, where the technician runs the fiber drop to the demarcation point, installs the ONT and configures the Business Gateway, typically take 2 to 4 hours.

## How soon can you install after I order?
The earliest installation date depends on your service zone; the serviceability check shows the estimated install lead time for your address. Appointments can be booked from the next business day onward, subject to technician availability.

## What appointment windows are available?
Installations are booked in a morning window from 8 AM to 12 PM or an afternoon window from 1 PM to 5 PM. You can reschedule or cancel an appointment before the technician is dispatched.

## Do you offer self-install kits?
No. Every business installation is performed by a Connectivity Max technician so the circuit can be tested and activated on site.

## Is installation free?
Standard professional installation is included at no charge with Business Fiber and Business Internet plans.

## Do I need to be on site?
An authorized contact must be on site for the full appointment window and provide access to the equipment room or demarcation point.

## When do I pay relative to installation?
The order flow is: cart, order, installation scheduling, then payment. Your installation appointment is booked first and payment is taken right after to confirm the order.
